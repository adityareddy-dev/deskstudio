# House style

The studio's own taste. It applies whenever the brand file or the user has not said otherwise, and it is what keeps a first run from looking like a template.

**Restraint.** One idea per screen. If a second idea arrives, it gets its own screen or it goes. Fewer elements, bigger.

**Type does the work.** One typeface, two weights, a scale with a ratio (1.25 for dense, 1.333 for display). The headline is big enough to read from across the room. Body is never under 16px on a screen, never under 24px on a social card. Headlines are tight, body is open. No letterspacing on lowercase text. No centred paragraphs longer than two lines.

**Space is the grid.** An 8px grid, every margin and gap a multiple of it. Groups read as groups because of space. The outer margin on a card is at least 6% of the short side, more on portrait. Nothing touches an edge except a bleed image or a deliberate full-bleed block.

**Colour has a job.** A background, a text colour, one accent for one purpose (the action, the number, the thing to look at). Grey is for secondary text, not for everything. No gradients unless the brand owns one. Dark and light are both designed, not inverted.

**Composition has a shape.** Asymmetry with intent. Left-aligned by default. A focal point in the top third or on the left third. The layout fits the aspect ratio it was made for: portrait stacks, landscape splits. A box inside a box is a last resort.

**Real copy.** The headline makes a claim or states a fact. No "Welcome", "Introducing", "seamless", "robust", "leverage", "unlock", "elevate", "supercharge". The CTA says what happens next. Numbers are plain figures. Nothing is placeholder.

**Motion is a sentence.** One movement per beat, 200 to 500ms for UI, ease-out in, ease-in out, nothing bounces unless the brand is playful. Every text block holds still long enough to read it twice. Reduced motion is respected.

**Banned** until a brand or a user asks for them: emoji as icons, drop shadows on flat UI, two gradients, three typefaces, stock gradient blobs, decorative blur circles, glass effects, every element as a card, default system fonts when a brand names one, lorem ipsum, an icon where a word would do.
