# Ferro

A desk-sized CNC mill for people who make hardware at home. Steel, not plastic.

## Voice

Blunt, technical, confident. Example: "0.01mm. On your desk."

## Do

- White background, black type, red only on the number or the action.
- Hard edges, heavy headlines, tight tracking.
- Specs as plain figures.

## Don't

- Rounded cards, soft shadows, pastel.
- Adjectives where a number would do.
