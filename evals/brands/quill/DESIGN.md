# Quill

A writing app that keeps drafts, not versions. Every sentence you cut is still there.

## Voice

Quiet, literary, exact. Example: "Cut it. It stays."

## Do

- Serif headlines, large, with air.
- Dark ink background, one pale blue accent on a word or a rule.
- Short lines, editorial rhythm.

## Don't

- Feature grids, icons, screenshots with chrome.
- Exclamation marks.
