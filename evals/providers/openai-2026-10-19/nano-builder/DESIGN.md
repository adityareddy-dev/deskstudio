# Northlight

Home solar that pays for itself in seven years, with a battery that keeps the lights on.

## Voice

Warm, plain, numerate. Example: "Seven years to break even. Then free."

## Do

- Lead with a number when there is one.
- Warm paper background, one orange accent on the figure or the action.
- Left aligned, generous margins.

## Don't

- Sky gradients, sun icons, leaves.
- More than one accent.
