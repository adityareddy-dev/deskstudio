08

Rules

Voice: warm, patient, plainspoken.

Do

  • Set the headline in Source Serif 4 600 and everything else in Inter 400.
  • Use the accent for the monogram and for one action per screen.
  • Put a paper label on a teal button in light, a charcoal label on accent-dark in dark.
  • Set every amount in Inter with tabular lining numerals.
  • Group with space on the 8px grid before reaching for a border.
Pick your payday

Don't

  • Don't use the accent for body text, large fills or decoration.
  • Don't set teal text on accent-soft, or muted-dark on accent-soft-dark.
  • Don't add a second accent, a gradient, a glow or a drop shadow on flat UI.
  • Don't put the serif on a number, a control or a paragraph.
  • Don't use coins, piggy banks, rising arrows, anchors, ship wheels or wave squiggles.
Pick your payday
